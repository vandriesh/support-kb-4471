The 5 one-day reports are filed under parser, so they count there too.

  1. parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so the day and the month change places: “7 April” becomes “4 July”. This is the bug that #4471 describes. The queued fix is for this problem.
  2. hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first. The form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3 have this problem. A parser fix does not change the hint.
  3. one-day. The date moves by one day, not by months. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” “Every booking in June is one day off.” This looks like a time zone or daylight saving bug. The desk noted these reports. They stay filed and counted under parser, but they need their own fix.

Why this happened

  • Decision 0001 merges reports by subject line. The subjects of all three problems are the same: “Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”. The subject tells you the symptom. The tenant, the form and the body tell you the cause.
  • Intake tags (meta.intakeTag) are a guess from the reporter’s browser. They disagree with the cause in both directions. Do not use them to split a ticket.
  • The ticket export has overlapping pages, so some reports come twice. Count unique ids only.
